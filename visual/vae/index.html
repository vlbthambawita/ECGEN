    VAE — Visual Guide¶

    VAE1D compresses a 12-lead ECG into a short sequence of Gaussian latent vectors and reconstructs it. After training, decoding random draws from N(0, I) generates new ECGs. Code: src/ecgen/families/vae/, loss: src/ecgen/families/vae/losses.py. Written reference: VAE.

    How the loss is computed¶

    total, recon, kl = vae_loss(recon, x, mean, logvar, kl_weight=1e-4)
    \[ \mathcal{L} \;=\; \underbrace{\frac{1}{BCL}\sum (\hat{x}-x)^2}_{\texttt{recon\_loss}} \;+\; \texttt{kl\_weight}\cdot \underbrace{\frac{-\tfrac12\sum\left(1+\log\sigma^2-\mu^2-\sigma^2\right)}{B\cdot C\cdot L}}_{\texttt{kl\_loss}} \]

    What kl_weight does¶

    The KL term pulls each ECG's posterior q(z | x) towards the prior N(0, I). The sketch below is schematic (one latent dimension, four ECGs); it is not computed from a trained model.
