    Visual Guide¶

    This section explains every implemented model family with diagrams: what goes in, what the network does to the tensor, what comes out, and how the training loss is computed. Most diagrams are animated or interactive. Hover over (or tap) a block to see the exact layer, and use the controls under a diagram to change its inputs.

    Page Model Generates from
    VAE VAE1D / VAELightning a Gaussian latent sequence z ~ N(0, I)
    VQ-VAE + Prior VQVAE1D + PixelCNNPrior code indices sampled by an autoregressive prior
    Pulse2Pulse GAN WaveGANGenerator + WaveGANDiscriminator uniform noise with the shape of an ECG
    Diffusion (DDPM) DiffusionModule + DDPMProcess + UNet1d Gaussian noise (n, 12, 5000), denoised in ~50 DDIM steps
    Flow Matching FlowMatchingModule + RectifiedFlowProcess + UNet1d Gaussian noise, moved along a learned velocity field with Euler / Heun steps

    All shapes and hyper-parameters in the diagrams are the defaults in src/ecgen/families/ and src/ecgen/nn/. The written reference for each model is under Models (VAE, VQ-VAE, Pulse2Pulse, Diffusion and flow matching).

    The input: a 12-lead ECG¶

    Every model works on a tensor of shape (batch, leads, samples). MIMICIVECG yields 12 leads × 5000 samples (10 s at 500 Hz) by default. Pulse2Pulse uses 8 channels (num_channels=8, selected with leads: independent8: I, II, V1–V6).

    Shared backbone: Encoder1D / Decoder1D¶

    The VAE and the VQ-VAE use the same convolutional encoder and decoder from src/ecgen/nn/. They differ only in the latent bottleneck: the VAE samples a Gaussian latent with 8 channels, and the VQ-VAE snaps a 64-channel latent to a codebook. Switch between them below:

    How long is the latent?¶

    Only the first len(channel_multipliers) − 1 stages downsample, each by 2 (Conv1d(k=4, stride=2, padding=1)), and the decoder upsamples by the same factor. If the input length is not divisible by the factor the reconstruction comes out shorter than the input:

    Use VQVAELightning.downsample_factor / latent_length() in code instead of hard-coding 8.
